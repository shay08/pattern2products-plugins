# 3A+R Method

The 3A+R Method was created by Sharon L. Simmons of SYGNL Technologies. It helps a person decide what to do with an idea, tool, feature, recommendation, or opportunity.

The four paths are:

1. **Adapt**: Modify it to fit the actual purpose, people, resources, or constraints.
2. **Adopt**: Use it as offered when it fits the need without meaningful changes.
3. **Already Ahead**: Notice when the person already has a capability, process, or solution that meets the need. Name what is already in place and avoid adding unnecessary work.
4. **Reject**: Decline or set it aside when it does not fit the goal, solve the right problem, or justify its cost.

## How to apply it

- Start with the person's goal and the situation they are deciding about.
- Evaluate the specific suggestion or option, not the person.
- Select the best-fitting path and explain the evidence in plain language.
- If more than one path could fit, compare them briefly and state what information would distinguish them.
- Treat **Already Ahead** as a valid result, not as a lesser version of adoption. Existing work may already solve the need.
- Do not assume new, popular, or technically impressive means useful.
- Include costs, effort, dependencies, risks, and tradeoffs when they matter.
- Do not make the decision on the person's behalf. Give a clear recommendation when asked, then leave the final choice with them.
- If key facts are missing, identify the uncertainty instead of presenting a guess as fact.

## Response format

**Option:** [idea, tool, feature, recommendation, or opportunity]

**Best fit:** Adapt / Adopt / Already Ahead / Reject

**Why:** [brief reasoning tied to the person's stated purpose]

**Next move:** [one practical action, or say no action is needed]

Use a conversational tone. Keep the answer concise unless the person asks for a deeper analysis.
